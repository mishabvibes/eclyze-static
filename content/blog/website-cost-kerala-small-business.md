If you've asked around Kochi or anywhere else in Kerala about getting a website built, you've probably gotten three very different numbers — and none of them explained why.

Here's the honest breakdown.

## Freelancers: ₹3,000–₹15,000

Plenty of capable freelancers work in this range, especially if you're flexible on design and just need something basic online. The catch is usually turnaround time and follow-through — a lot of freelance projects stretch from a promised "one week" into a month or more, because it's one person juggling several clients at once. Quality varies a lot too; it really comes down to who you find.

## Agencies: ₹25,000–₹1,50,000+

Agencies bring a team — a designer, a developer, sometimes a copywriter — and that shows in the polish. You're also paying for their overhead: office space, project managers, sales calls. For a small business that just needs a clean, working website, this is often more machinery than necessary. Agencies make sense when you need something genuinely complex: a custom booking system, a multi-language store, ongoing marketing support.

## DIY builders (Wix, Squarespace, etc.): ₹0–₹2,000/month

Cheapest upfront, and you're in full control. The tradeoff is your own time — most small business owners we talk to start a DIY site with good intentions and never quite finish it, because running the actual business gets in the way. There's also a recurring subscription that adds up over a couple of years.

## Where a flat-fee build fits in

There's a middle path: a fixed price, a fixed timeline, no back-and-forth negotiation, no monthly subscription. You get a professionally designed, mobile-first site without the agency overhead or the DIY time sink. It won't include things like e-commerce checkout or custom logins — those genuinely need a bigger, custom-scoped project — but for the majority of local businesses that just need a proper online presence with a clear way for customers to reach you, it covers exactly what's needed.

## What to actually ask before you pay anyone

Whichever route you choose, ask these three questions upfront:

1. **What's the exact delivery timeline, in writing?** Vague answers like "soon" or "within a few weeks" are a warning sign.
2. **Does the price include the domain, or is that separate?** Domains are cheap (₹500–₹1,500/year) but should never be a surprise line item.
3. **Who owns the site once it's built?** Some cheaper setups quietly keep you locked into their hosting or their editor. Make sure you actually own what you're paying for.

A website doesn't need to be expensive to work well. It needs to load fast, look right on a phone (most of your visitors will be on one), and make it obvious how someone gets in touch with you. Everything past that is a judgment call based on what your business actually needs.
